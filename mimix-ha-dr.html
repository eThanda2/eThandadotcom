Service Deep Dive

MIMIX High Availability

Zero-data-loss architecture. We configure, test, and manage Precisely MIMIX replication to ensure absolute business continuity across your IBM i LPARs.

Real-Time Logical Replication

We implement Precisely MIMIX Availability to continuously replicate data, objects, and IFS changes from your production IBM i server to a backup recovery server in real-time.

  • Journal-Based Replication: Ensuring sub-second RPO (Recovery Point Objective) by tracking OS/400 journal receivers.
  • Data Auditing: Configuring rule-based audits to prevent silent out-of-sync conditions between production and target nodes.

LPAR to LPAR Sync

Continuous, automated synchronization across nodes.

Virtual Role Swaps & Test Benches

A disaster recovery plan is only as good as its last successful test. We specialize in configuring Virtual Role Swaps to test failover readiness without disrupting the live production environment.

Simulated Failovers

We execute safe, isolated tests on the target server to verify that the target database can assume the production role instantly during an actual outage.

Automated Recovery

Developing robust runbooks and automated scripts to handle DNS switching, subsystem startup, and user redirection during a planned or unplanned swap.
